# Room for Everly

A 3D model of Everly's home, built from her hand-drawn top-view sketch, in pastel pink with a My Melody theme.

**Live:** https://antantantant.github.io/room-for-everly/

## The home

East is the left of the sketch, so every window faces the sunrise:

- **Living room, kitchen and dining** (top of the sketch, the south side): two pink sofas (22, 25), a coffee table (26), the TV (24) on its stand (23) with toys underneath, the kitchen (21) with its cooktop and hood, and a dining table (20) with four bunny-eared chairs (16–19).
- **Main bedroom**: the bed (1) under the east window, a wardrobe (2), a bedside table and lamp (3), a floor lamp (4), and a desk with a laptop (6). A short hallway leads to the living room, and door 5 opens into a little hall to the kids' room.
- **The big bathroom**: a vanity with the sink (14), floss and toothbrushes; a toilet room (15) with a small basin and a paper holder; a glass shower; and two walk-in closets (12, 13), each with a mirror.
- **Kids' room**: a bunk bed (9), a table (10) and two wardrobes (7, 8), with a My Melody plush on each bunk.

The parts of the sketch marked "not in" are left open, like courtyards.

## Everly's squirrel

A squirrel in a pink dress, from Everly's front and back drawings, made in the soft, round style of Animal Crossing. She lives in the kids' room.

- **Walk her with the arrow keys** (or W A S D). Up walks away from you and left walks to the screen's left, whichever way you are looking. The camera follows her.
- She swings her arms and feet as she walks, and her tail waves all the time.
- She walks round furniture and through doorways but not through walls. The **Squirrel** button jumps to a view just behind her.

## Things to try

- Press **Begin the sunrise**. It starts one minute before the sun comes up, with music, and the living-room window is open to the breeze.
- **Click a lamp** to switch it on or off: the bedside lamp, the bedroom floor lamp, the bathroom's vanity lights and the kids' desk lamp.
- Use the buttons to jump between rooms, or drag and scroll to look around.

Built with three.js: real-time shadows, ambient occlusion, light beams through the windows, soft fur on the plush toys and fabrics, and generated music.
