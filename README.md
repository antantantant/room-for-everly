# Room for Everly

A 3D model of Everly's house, built from her hand-drawn top-view sketch, in pastel pink with a My Melody theme.

**Live:** https://antantantant.github.io/room-for-everly/

## The house

It is a one-storey house standing in its own garden on top of a hill: the windows look out over the lawn, a hedge, round trees and the neighbours' cottages, with the castle beyond. From the backyard you see the back of the house: pink-and-cream clapboard, shuttered windows with flower boxes, a lantern by the back door, and a rose-pink shingle roof with a chimney (the roof only shows while you're out in the yard, so it never hides the rooms).

East is the left of the sketch, so every window faces the sunrise:

- **Living room, kitchen and dining** (top of the sketch, the south side): two pink sofas (22, 25), a coffee table (26), the TV (24) on its stand (23) with toys underneath, the kitchen (21) with its cooktop and hood, and a dining table (20) with four bunny-eared chairs (16–19).
- **Main bedroom**: the bed (1) under the east window, a wardrobe (2), a bedside table and lamp (3), a floor lamp (4), and a desk with a laptop (6). A short hallway leads to the living room, and door 5 opens into a little hall to the kids' room.
- **The big bathroom**: a vanity with the sink (14), floss and toothbrushes; a toilet room (15) with a small basin and a paper holder; a glass shower; and two walk-in closets (12, 13), each with a mirror.
- **Kids' room**: a bunk bed (9), a table (10) and two wardrobes (7, 8), with a My Melody plush on each bunk.
- **Backyard**: through the back door beside the fridge (it starts shut; Space opens it): a lawn behind a white picket fence, stepping stones, a swing, a little apple tree, a flower bed, a ball and a watering can. The Backyard button shows it.

The parts of the sketch marked "not in" are left open, like courtyards.

## Everly's squirrel

A squirrel in a pink dress, from Everly's front and back drawings, made in the soft, round style of Animal Crossing. She lives in the kids' room.

- **Walk her with the arrow keys** (or W A S D). Up walks away from you and left walks to the screen's left, whichever way you are looking. The camera follows her.
- She swings her arms and feet as she walks, and her tail waves all the time.
- She walks round furniture and through doorways but not through walls. The **Squirrel** button jumps to a view just behind her.
- **Space** uses whatever is glowing next to her (its name shows in a label):
  - picks things up: cushions, pillows, plushes, toys, cups and plates, the kettle, cupcakes, clothes, chairs, plants and more. Small things are carried in front of her, big ones over her head. Space again puts it down in front of her, on a table if there is one there.
  - opens and shuts the doors, windows (the curtains blow when one is open), wardrobes and the pink fridge (with food inside to take out), and switches the lamps and the TV on and off.
- **R** plays with whatever has a pink **P** bubble over it. Each press picks one of its activities at random, for example:
  - bunk bed: sleep or sit on the lower bunk, or climb the ladder and sleep or jump on the top bunk
  - big bed: sleep, sit on the edge, bounce, hug Melody · sofas: sit, nap, bounce, look out at the castle
  - kitchen: stir the saucepan, boil the kettle, wash hands or a plate, grab a snack or milk from the fridge, eat a cupcake
  - bathroom: brush teeth, wash face, floss, funny faces in the mirrors, use the toilet, wash hands at the little basin, take a shower
  - backyard: swing (or swing really high), bounce, kick or throw the big ball (it bounces and stays where it lands), smell or water the flowers, shake or hug the apple tree, watch the clouds, cartwheels, roll in the grass, bunny hops
  - desks: type or watch a video on the laptop, draw, colour, doodle and show it off · wardrobes: try on clothes (a new dress for a while) or hide · lit-up clothes: R puts them on
  - rugs: dance, roll around, bunny hops · the TV, toys, chairs, pouf, plants, plushes, doors, the floor lamp, the backpack and the kids' window too
  - Any arrow key stops an activity early. Sitting down, she stays seated until an arrow key gets her up.
- **Energy**: playing costs energy (bouncing and dancing most), walking a little. Sleeping fills it up and snacks help. When it runs low she gets sleepy and won't play until she has had a nap. Doing the same thing three times in a row bores her.
- **L** sends the thing she moved last back to where it started, even out of her hands. Press it again for the one before, and so on.
- **Feelings**: she is happy when she picks something up or switches something on, sad when she puts down a plush or the TV goes off, cross if she keeps walking into things, bored if left alone, and then sleepy. The feeling buttons (or keys 1 to 5) set one directly.

## Things to try

- Press **Begin the sunrise**. It starts one minute before the sun comes up, with music (an original celesta waltz), and the living-room window is open to the breeze.
- Look out of the windows: a fairytale castle stands on a crag across the valley, above a lake, its windows glowing.
- **Click a lamp** to switch it on or off: the bedside lamp, the bedroom floor lamp, the bathroom's vanity lights and the kids' desk lamp.
- Use the buttons to jump between rooms, or drag and scroll to look around.

Built with three.js: real-time shadows, ambient occlusion, light beams through the windows, soft fur on the plush toys and fabrics, and generated music.
